2. Information You Provide

Todo App allows you to create and manage tasks.

Depending on the features you use, information you enter into the App may include:

  • Task names
  • Categories
  • Due dates
  • Due times
  • Reminder settings
  • Priority
  • Repeat settings
  • Task descriptions
  • Subtasks
  • Task completion status

This information is used to provide the task-management and reminder functionality of the App.

3. Local Storage

Your task information is primarily stored locally on your device using the App's local storage.

Task information stored locally includes information such as your tasks, categories, reminders, descriptions, subtasks, and completion status.

We do not operate a server that receives or stores the task information you enter into the App.

If you uninstall the App or clear its application data, locally stored information may be deleted.

4. Notifications and Reminders

Todo App may request permission to send notifications.

If you enable task reminders, the App uses notification functionality to notify you at the date and time associated with your reminder settings.

Notification permission can be managed or disabled through your device's Android settings.

The App does not require notification permission for basic task-management functionality.

9. Information We Do Not Collect Through the App

The App does not require you to create an account to use its core task-management functionality.

We do not intentionally collect through the App:

  • Your call history
  • Your passwords
  • Your financial account information

The App only requests permissions necessary for the features it provides.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell your task information.

We do not share the task information stored locally on your device with third parties because we do not operate a server that receives that task information.

However, third-party services integrated into the App, such as Google AdMob and Google Play services, may independently collect and process information as described in their applicable policies.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App.

Because task information is stored locally on your device, the security of that information also depends on the security of your device.

We recommend using appropriate Android device security measures, such as a screen lock, password, PIN, or biometric authentication.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Task information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

You can delete individual tasks using the App's task-management features.

Because the App does not maintain a server-side account containing your tasks, there is no separate server account that needs to be deleted.

Information that may be processed by third-party services, such as advertising or Google Play services, is subject to the respective third party's retention and deletion practices.
